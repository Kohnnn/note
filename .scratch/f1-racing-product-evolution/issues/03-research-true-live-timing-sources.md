# Research true live timing sources and obligations

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Which authorized primary data sources could support genuine F1 live timing for this product, and what do their official terms and interfaces imply for licensing, redistribution, attribution, authentication, latency, completeness, geographic restrictions, reliability, cost, and historical-replay handoff? Separate technically observable facts from assumptions and reject any option whose production rights cannot be established.

## Answer

[Research artifact](../research/03-true-live-timing-sources.md)

No reviewed option establishes both an authorized public live-data product and adequate production rights and operating terms. OpenF1 is technically closest but its published license is non-commercial and does not establish this product's redistribution, retention, replay, SLA, geography, or rights chain. FastF1 records an F1TV stream for post-session use and explicitly cannot process it in real time; its software license grants no upstream-data rights. True live timing must stay out of the release train until every rights, commercial, provenance, reliability, and live-to-replay proof gate in the artifact passes.
