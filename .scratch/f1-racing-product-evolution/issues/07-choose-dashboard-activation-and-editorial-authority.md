# Choose the Dashboard activation promise and editorial authority

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 06

## Question

What single first-session outcome should the Unified Dashboard promise an engineering learner, and who owns the claims it presents? Choose among understanding one race outcome, one engineering subsystem through race evidence, or one driver comparison; then define which statements are recorded, derived, editorial, or illustrative and what review is required before featuring them.

## Answer

The Unified Dashboard promises to teach one engineering subsystem through recorded race evidence. A featured brief starts with a subsystem question, opens exact Replay evidence, explains the mechanism through the matching Learn module or Modelview focus, and ends at an explicit evidence ceiling rather than forcing a causal conclusion.

Briefs are pipeline-generated and may publish automatically only through versioned, deterministic, allowlisted templates. Every statement is typed as `Recorded`, `Derived`, or `Unknown`; illustrative Modelview and wind content cannot become session evidence. Generation must fail closed when required anchors, provenance, coverage, or uncertainty fields are absent, and templates must prohibit unsupported causal language. Free-form generated interpretation is outside this authority model.

The pilot set is Monza qualifying braking-zone versus exit, Mexico race whole-lap advantage without a fabricated single cause, and Zandvoort race safety-car interruption. Together they prove Braking, Aero/evidence discipline, and Strategy/Tyres without requiring new upstream data.
