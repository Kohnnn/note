# Define the roles and disclosure of core experiences

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 07, 08

## Question

What job, default depth, and handoff belongs to Replay Story, Replay Workspace, Learn, Modelview, 2D track, optional Replay 3D, wind visualizer, Compare, and Stints? Define beginner defaults, advanced disclosures, evidence links, context preservation, and stopping boundaries so every surface supports the same learner journey without duplicating ownership.

## Answer

The one learner journey is Dashboard question → exact Replay evidence → Learn explanation or Modelview focus → explicit evidence ceiling and next step. Preserve the validated Replay URL and selected focus at every handoff; browser-local Learn progress may resume locally. No account, duplicate content model, or new peer product surface is authorized.

- **Dashboard:** The sole canonical entry (`/`) selects one engineering subsystem and presents the deterministic brief. Default to the learner question, exact evidence link, `Recorded`/`Derived`/`Unknown` labels, matching explanation, and ceiling. It owns activation and editorial framing, not replay controls, instruction, or analysis. It must not call Modelview, wind, or unmeasured mechanisms session evidence, invent causality, or become a control room.
- **Replay Story:** The beginner default within an exact session. It pauses on a small sequence of existing, labeled evidence anchors and hands off to Workspace for inspection. It owns guided orientation only; every step links to its underlying frame, timeline, comparison, or stated derivation. It must not replace the brief, conceal provenance/coverage, or turn derived annotations into adjudicated analysis.
- **Replay Workspace:** The canonical evidence surface at `/replay/{season}/{grandPrix}/{session}`. It owns playback, source/position-coverage disclosure, track state, telemetry, race control, and in-context Compare/Stints. Default track view is accessible 2D; selected drivers, time, tab, and focus remain shareable in the Replay URL. It must preserve the distinction between GPS-projected, projected, and published positions; not claim continuous ground truth, named-corner attribution without verified mapping, pass detection, literal brake points, causal strategy outcomes, or true-live status.
- **Historical Race Desk:** A clearly labeled accelerated historical replay simulation, entered after the primary journey. It owns a static control-room view of the latest historical pack, not evidence authority or live coverage. Its disclosure says: “Historical replay simulation — not live timing.” It must never imply current, real-time, licensed, or authoritative live timing; `/live` remains only its compatibility redirect.
- **Learn:** The durable, conceptual explanation surface for the existing six modules. Default to the relevant focused module and its local progress; it owns concepts, terminology, and the learner’s read/completion state, not session conclusions. Link back to the exact Replay evidence and, where useful, Modelview focus. It must distinguish general engineering explanation from a claim about the selected session and not add new topics before the six modules meet the later learning contract.
- **Modelview:** The optional car-orientation and subsystem-context surface. Default to the brief’s named focus, with static-reference disclosure and a return link to the preserved Replay state. It owns component location and generic mechanism prompts, not car-specific setup, sensor data, or race measurement. Its airflow copy is static reference text; it must not validate a session aero, braking, tyre, or setup claim.
- **2D track and Replay 3D:** 2D is the default, accessible evidence view. Replay 3D is an optional, progressively disclosed orientation or camera aid only when it materially aids understanding; it must have an equivalent 2D path and cannot be required to inspect evidence. Both inherit the Workspace’s position-coverage disclosure and cannot prove overtakes, continuous GPS paths, or named-corner handling without the missing validation.
- **Wind visualizer:** A separately disclosed educational 2D solver. Default hidden behind Modelview’s advanced exploration; label forces and coefficients as estimates and state “not validated CFD.” It owns illustrative flow exploration only. It must never be presented as session-specific airflow, quantitative race evidence, validated CFD, or proof of drag, downforce, wing, setup, or performance claims.
- **Compare:** A Replay-owned analysis capability, not a product pillar. Standalone routes remain for discovery and bookmarks, but Dashboard links open the matching Replay comparison state when that is the clearer continuation. It owns side-by-side selected-lap recorded traces and clearly derived annotations; it must identify telemetry sampling and not turn threshold events, sector windows, DRS state, or aggregate timing into literal brake points, named corners, force measurements, or causal engineering conclusions.
- **Stints:** A Replay-owned analysis capability, with stable standalone routes for discovery and bookmarks. It owns recorded stint boundaries and explicitly derived pace/strategy summaries; its primary handoff is the matching Replay stints or race-control state. It must label slopes as recorded-lap pace trends, label pit-loss/SC-VSC outputs as estimates, segment neutralisations, and not claim degradation, cheap stops, undercut/overcut wins, tyre warm-up, team intent, or measured pit loss without the missing evidence.

This rejects the prior Modelview-wind-tunnel-to-race-proof, track-map-overtaking-proof, named-corner-diagnosis, Monaco-best-pit-window, and component-performance cases: each needs data or validation the current packs do not supply. The first three Dashboard pilots remain the Monza braking, Mexico whole-lap, and Zandvoort safety-car briefs; they reuse these roles rather than creating alternate stories.
