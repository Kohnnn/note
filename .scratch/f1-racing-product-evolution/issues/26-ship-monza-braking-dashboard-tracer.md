# 26 — Ship the Monza Braking Dashboard tracer

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: 09, 10, 11, 12

**What to build:** A learner can open the Unified Dashboard, choose Braking, follow a pipeline-generated Monza qualifying brief into exact Replay evidence, continue into the Braking explanation and brake-focused Modelview context, and understand which observations are recorded, derived, or unknown.

- [ ] The approved Dashboard composition presents Braking as a clear subsystem choice and explains the expected learning outcome before opening evidence.
- [ ] The generator emits the approved Monza driver/lap/sector and braking anchors from current packs through a deterministic, versioned template.
- [ ] Every claim is visibly typed as Recorded, Derived, or Unknown, and missing provenance, coverage, anchor, or uncertainty data fails generation.
- [ ] The brief opens the exact Replay comparison state and offers context-preserving next steps to the Braking module and brake-focused Modelview experience.
- [ ] The brief never equates sampled brake thresholds with literal brake points or infers brake balance, temperature, setup, driver intent, or causality.
- [ ] The complete Dashboard-to-evidence-to-explanation path is keyboard-accessible, responsive, shareable where approved, and covered by deterministic data assertions and a browser acceptance test.
