# Interactive Explanation Atlas

A curated catalog and learning guide for self-directed learners to find, use, and continue through locally hosted interactive explanations.

## Language

**Atlas**:
The complete discovery surface for finding and returning to interactive routes.
_Avoid_: Archive, gallery, library

**Route**:
One independently launchable interactive explanation, simulation, practice activity, creation tool, or guided path.
_Avoid_: Site, page, replica

**Guided Path**:
An advisory, curated sequence of related routes with an explicit next step.
_Avoid_: Course, curriculum, learning track

**Progress**:
A learner's explicit, local-only position within a Guided Path, retained for 30 days.
_Avoid_: Completion analytics, account history

**Learning Guide**:
The Atlas behavior that helps a learner choose a Route and identify the next useful Route without becoming a learning-management system.
_Avoid_: LMS, personalized recommendation engine

**Suggested Next Route**:
One editorially chosen Route offered as the next useful step after any Route. It is advisory and creates Progress only when it is part of a Guided Path.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, autoplay queue, personalized next step
